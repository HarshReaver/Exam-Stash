Computer Networks Question Bank (Modules 4, 5, and 6)

Module 4: The Network Layer

2 Marks Questions:

  1. Define IPv4 and IPv6.
  2. What is subnetting?
  3. Define routing and its importance.
  4. What is fragmentation in networking?
  5. Define the term "hop count."

5 Marks Questions:

  1. Explain various routing algorithms in detail.
  2. Describe the importance of address resolution and discuss ARP.
  3. Compare different routing techniques.
  4. Explain the working of ICMP and its significance.
  5. Discuss CIDR and its benefits in IP addressing.

10 Marks Questions:

  1. Explain the process of IP forwarding in detail.
  2. Discuss IPv4 vs. IPv6 addressing in depth.
  3. Explain in detail the working of distance vector and link-state routing protocols.
  4. Describe NAT (Network Address Translation) and its applications.

Module 5: The Transport Layer ⚡

2 Marks Questions:

  1. Define TCP and UDP.
  2. What is congestion control?
  3. What is meant by flow control?
  4. Define multiplexing and demultiplexing in the transport layer.
  5. What is the role of the transport layer in end-to-end communication?

5 Marks Questions:

  1. Explain the working of TCP three-way handshake.
  2. Discuss transport layer protocols with their significance.
  3. Explain the differences between TCP and UDP.
  4. Describe the concept of reliable data transfer in TCP.
  5. Explain the importance of port numbers in transport layer communication.

10 Marks Questions:

  1. Explain in detail the working of TCP and UDP, their features, and differences.
  2. Discuss the TCP congestion control mechanisms in detail.
  3. Explain the concept of flow control and congestion control in TCP.
  4. Discuss the working of SCTP (Stream Control Transmission Protocol).

Module 6: The Application Layer

2 Marks Questions:

  1. Define HTTP and FTP.
  2. What is DNS?
  3. What are the different email protocols?
  4. Define the term "client-server architecture."
  5. What is the significance of cookies in HTTP?

5 Marks Questions:

  1. Explain the working of DNS in detail.
  2. Discuss the importance of email protocols (SMTP, POP3, IMAP).
  3. Explain the functioning of HTTP and HTTPS.
  4. Describe the working of FTP and its modes.
  5. Explain the role of the Application Layer in networking.

10 Marks Questions:

  1. Explain in detail the working of the application layer protocols such as WWW, DNS, and FTP.
  2. Discuss the role and working of email protocols in depth.
  3. Explain client-server and peer-to-peer architectures with examples.
  4. Describe in detail the security issues in the application layer and their solutions.

Note: This document includes all questions from Modules 4, 5, and 6, arranged based on marks distribution. ✅
